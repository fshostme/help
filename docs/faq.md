# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

::: details What is FSHOST.me?
FSHOST.me is a game server hosting platform that offers both free and paid (Pro) plans. We support various popular games (mostly FPS) and provide easy-to-use tools for managing your game servers.
:::

::: details Do I need to create an account?
Yes, you need to create a free account to use FSHOST.me services, whether you're using free or Pro servers.
:::

::: details What games are supported?
Our most popular games are Counter-Strike 2, Counter-Strike 1.6, and Call of Duty 4. Check out our website [Games](https://fshost.me/games) page for the complete list of supported games.
:::

## Free Servers

::: details How long can I use a free server?
Free servers stay online for as long as you are playing on them. Join within 5 minutes of creating the server, or it goes offline. Once everyone leaves, the server stops on its own.

Servers that are kept online with idling players are stopped, and repeated idling can get your account blocked.
:::

::: details Can I upgrade my free server to Pro?
No. Free and Pro servers are separate. Rent a Pro server from the [Pro Panel](https://fshost.me/pro/servers) and set it up there. You can keep using free servers after you go Pro.
:::

::: details Are there any limitations on free servers?
Yes. You can run one free server at a time, with up to 14 player slots on most games. Free servers have basic settings, pre-defined plugins and mods, no FTP, and an ad in the server hostname. Not every location takes free servers. See [Free vs Pro](/servers/free-vs-pro) for the full comparison.
:::

## Pro Servers

::: details How much do Pro servers cost?
Pro server pricing varies by game. The price includes up to 18 player slots, which you set in the Pro Panel. If you need more, ask on a [support ticket](/servers/pro/support). On CS2 and CS 1.6, extra slots cost €0.50 per slot per month. See the [pricing page](https://fshost.me/pro/pricing) for current prices.
:::

::: details What payment methods do you accept?
Payments go through Stripe. You can pay with card, PayPal and other methods, depending on your country. Amounts are charged in euros. See [Billing and Top-Up](/servers/pro/billing#top-up-your-balance) for how to add money to your balance.
:::

::: details Can I cancel my Pro server subscription?
We don't currently run a subscription for servers. All payments are manual top-ups by you so we can't take any money without it being in your FSHOST account balance first.

A running server renews from your balance on its renewal date. To stop paying, stop the server. Stopped servers are not charged. See [Billing and Top-Up](/servers/pro/billing#how-charging-works).
:::

## Technical Questions

::: details What if I can't connect to my server?
Check out our [Connection Problems](/troubleshooting/connection) guide for troubleshooting steps.
:::

::: details How do I access my server console?
Pro servers have a **Console** tab in the Pro Panel. Free servers are managed with [RCON](/rcon) from the game. See [Console Access](/servers/pro/console) for more information.
:::

::: details Can I install custom plugins or mods?
Pro servers can use custom plugins and mods through FTP, on games that offer it. FTP is set up on request through a support ticket. See [FTP Access](/servers/pro/ftp). Free servers come with pre-defined plugins and mods only.
:::

::: details Where are your servers located?
We have servers in multiple locations worldwide. See our [Locations](/network/locations) page for the complete list.
:::

## Support

::: details How do I get help?
You can get help through:
- Our [Common Issues](/troubleshooting/) section
- Free servers: the `#free-support` channel on our [Discord](https://fshost.me/discord)
- Pro customers: support tickets on [Pro Support](https://fshost.me/pro/support), or the `#pro-support` channel on Discord. See [Support Tickets](/servers/pro/support)

Please write in English or Danish. Messages in other languages are not answered. The [Contact](https://fshost.me/contact) page lists our support hours.
:::

::: details How quickly do you respond to support requests?
Pro customers get the highest priority, usually within 10 minutes during support hours. We aim to answer every request within 1 hour during support hours, and within 12 hours at most outside them. Free support on Discord is answered when staff have time.

Support hours (UTC):

| Day | Hours |
|-----|-------|
| Monday to Saturday | 08:00 to 21:00 |
| Sunday | 10:00 to 19:00 |

The [Pro Support](https://fshost.me/pro/support) page shows whether support is online right now and how fast recent tickets were answered.
:::
