# Free vs Pro Servers

## Our Mission

We started FSHOST to help gamers play together without the barrier of expensive hosting and complex settings. Free servers will always be our foundation, it's in our name, and they're here to stay.

**Pro servers help us keep the lights on** and continue offering free hosting to everyone. When you go Pro, you also support a small team keeping free gaming accessible.

Both run on the same hardware. Pro adds more settings, customization, 24/7 uptime and priority support.

## Comparison

| | Free | Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Price | €0 | Monthly price per game, see [pricing](https://fshost.me/pro/pricing) |
| Online 24/7 | No, stops after everyone leaves | Yes |
| 99.9% uptime guarantee | No | Yes |
| Player slots | Up to 14 on most games | Up to 18, more on request |
| Servers at a time | 1 | 3, more on request |
| Server settings | Basic | Advanced |
| Plugins and mods | Pre-defined | Custom, varies by game |
| Custom maps | Community map pool | Add your own, varies by game |
| FTP access | No | On request, on selected games |
| Server backups | No | On request |
| Ad in server hostname | Yes | No |
| Ads on the website | Yes | No |
| Support | Discord `#free-support` | Tickets and Discord `#pro-support`, highest priority |
| DDoS protection | Basic or advanced, depends on location | Basic or advanced, depends on location |

Some games are Pro only, such as Counter-Strike: Global Offensive. Minecraft is available as a free server, but only in vanilla, without RCON or operator (op) access, and the world is not saved when the server stops. The [Games](https://fshost.me/games) page shows what each game offers.

## Free Servers

Free servers are free, with no payment details needed. They stop when you stop playing.

**What you get:**

- A full game server with basic settings and pre-defined plugins and mods
- One free server at a time, with up to 14 player slots on most games
- Help from the community and staff in `#free-support` on our [Discord](https://fshost.me/discord)

**Trade-offs:**

- You have 5 minutes to join after creating the server, or it goes offline
- The server stops on its own once it is empty
- Servers kept online by idling players are stopped, and repeated idling can get your account blocked
- Your server name ends with `- free @ fshost.me`, and the free server pages of the website show ads. Do not remove the hostname ad
- Not every location takes free servers, and each location has a limited number of free spaces. At busy times a location can be full

**Great for:**

- Playing with friends
- Testing and learning
- Casual gaming sessions

## Pro Servers

By choosing Pro, you help us keep offering free servers and improving the website.

**What you get:**

- A server that stays online 24/7 until you stop it, with a 99.9% uptime guarantee. See our [status page](https://status.fsho.st)
- Advanced settings in the [Pro Panel](https://fshost.me/pro/servers), with a console, schedules and [team access](/servers/pro/team-access)
- Custom plugins, mods and maps, and [FTP](/servers/pro/ftp) on request, depending on the game
- Up to 18 player slots, with more on request
- Crashed servers are restarted automatically
- Priority support through [tickets](/servers/pro/support), usually answered within 10 minutes during support hours
- No ads in the hostname or on the website

**Best for:**

- Active communities that rely on their server
- Competitive games, training and team matches
- Anyone who wants to support us

## Pricing

Pro servers start at €5 per month, and each game has its own monthly price. Once your server is created, its price does not change for the life of the server. See the **[Pro Pricing Page](https://fshost.me/pro/pricing)** for the price, locations and features of each game. Pro servers are paid from your account balance, with no subscription. See [Billing and Top-Up](/servers/pro/billing).

## Can I Go Pro Later?

Yes. Free and Pro servers are separate, so there is nothing to convert:

- You can still create free servers after going Pro
- A stopped Pro server is not charged. After it has been stopped for one month you get warning emails before it is deleted. See [Stopped Servers and Deletion](/servers/pro/billing#stopped-servers-and-deletion)
