# Getting Started

New to FSHOST.me? This page takes you from an account to a running game server, and points you to the right guide for each step.

## Step 1: Create an Account

Sign up at [fshost.me/register](https://fshost.me/register). You can also sign up with Google or Discord.

Once you are in, we recommend turning on two factor authentication. See [Account and Security](/account).

## Step 2: Choose Free or Pro

| | Free | Pro |
|---|------|-----|
| Cost | Free | Monthly price per server, paid from your balance |
| Runs | While people are playing on it | Until you stop it |
| Ads | Yes, in the hostname and chat | No |
| Support | Community, on Discord | Priority, with support tickets |
| Plugins and FTP | Predefined only | Yes, FTP on request |

Read [Free vs Pro](/servers/free-vs-pro) for the full comparison. Not sure yet? Start with a free server. You can rent a Pro server at any time.

## Step 3: Pick a Location

Choose the location closest to you and your players. Lower ping means a smoother game.

- [Locations](/network/locations) lists where our servers are
- [Ping Test](/network/ping-test) shows how to check your ping to each one

## Step 4: Create Your Server

### Free Server

1. Pick your game on the [Games](https://fshost.me/games) page
2. Choose a location, a hostname and the number of slots
3. Click **Create**
4. Join within 5 minutes, or the server goes offline

Full guide: [Creating a Free Server](/servers/free/creating-server)

### Pro Server

1. Add money to your balance on the [Top-up](https://fshost.me/pro/top-up) page
2. Pick your game on the [Pricing](https://fshost.me/pro/pricing) page and click **Create**
3. Choose a location and configure the server
4. Click **Start**. The first month's price is taken from your balance

Full guides: [Creating a Pro Server](/servers/pro/creating-server) and [Billing and Top-Up](/servers/pro/billing)

## Step 5: Connect and Play

The IP and port are shown at the top of your server's page. Open the game console and type:

```bash
connect IP:PORT
```

Share the same address with your friends. If the server has a join password, give them that too.

Can't connect? See [Connection Problems](/troubleshooting/connection).
